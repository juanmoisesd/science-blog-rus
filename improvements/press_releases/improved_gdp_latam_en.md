# Press Release Improvement: GDP Evolution Latin America (2000-2024)

**Original DOI:** 10.5281/zenodo.19287489
**Topic:** GDP Evolution in Latin America
**Language:** English

---

## 1. Suggested Headline
**Two Decades of Economic Shift: New Open Dataset Tracks GDP Evolution Across Latin America (2000-2024)**

## 2. Key Message
This open-access dataset provides a comprehensive longitudinal analysis of Gross Domestic Product (GDP) across Latin American nations over the last 24 years. It enables researchers, policymakers, and economists to perform secondary analyses on regional growth patterns, economic resilience, and the impact of global crises on local economies.

## 3. Key Findings (Based on Dataset Scope)
*   **Regional Growth Trends:** Identification of high-growth periods versus stagnation phases across the subcontinent.
*   **Volatility Mapping:** Data highlights the varying levels of economic stability between Andean, Southern Cone, and Central American regions.
*   **Crisis Impact:** Visual representation of how regional GDP responded to the 2008 financial crisis and the 2020 global pandemic.
*   **Comparative Metrics:** Standardized variables allow for direct country-to-country comparisons without additional normalization.

## 4. Why This Matters
Latin America’s economic landscape is often characterized by high volatility. Understanding long-term trends is crucial for sustainable development planning. This dataset bridges the gap between raw economic reports and actionable research-ready data, promoting transparency and evidence-based policy making in the region.

## 5. Dataset Overview & Methodology
*   **N (Countries):** Comprehensive coverage of Latin American nations.
*   **Time Span:** 2000 – 2024.
*   **Data Source:** Compiled and standardized from official regional and international economic databases (e.g., World Bank, ECLAC).
*   **Format:** Open CSV/XLSX for maximum interoperability.

## 6. Open Science & Transparency Statement
In alignment with FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable), this dataset is released under a CC0/CC-BY license. No new primary data were collected; instead, existing indicators were harmonized and cleaned to eliminate inconsistencies found in fragmented sources.

## 7. Potential Applications
*   **Econometrics:** Testing growth models in emerging markets.
*   **Public Policy:** Evaluating the long-term effectiveness of economic reforms.
*   **Education:** A real-world resource for university-level data science and economics courses.
*   **Data Journalism:** Creating interactive visualizations of regional economic history.
